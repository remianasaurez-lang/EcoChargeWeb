ABOUT ECOCHARGE

Built so your commute doesn't cost the planet

EcoCharge started with a simple frustration: EV owners were doing the right thing by going electric, then plugging into a grid that's still mostly coal. We wanted charging that was actually clean, not just quiet.

So every EcoCharge station generates and stores its own power on-site. You find it, you plug in, and you can see exactly where that energy came from — no guesswork, no greenwashing.

Generate where you charge. Solar panels on the canopy feed a battery bank right at the station, so power doesn't travel far to reach your car.

Store it for when you need it. Battery storage means stations keep charging after sunset and on cloudy days, not just at noon.

Show you the difference. Every session in the app tells you how much of your charge came from the sun.

Sun to socket, generated and stored right at the station — nothing pulled from the coal-heavy grid.

IMPACT

Small choice, real difference

You don't have to change how you drive to make a difference — just where you plug in.

Less pressure on the grid

Because each station generates and stores its own power, your charge draws on sunlight collected right there — not on peak-hour grid demand that's often met by burning more fossil fuel.

Charging keeps going after dark

The battery bank at every station stores the day's sunlight, so you're never stuck waiting for daylight — the sun you charge on might have set hours ago.

Why this matters: the electricity grid in most of India is still largely coal-fired. Every session run on stored solar is a session that didn't add to that demand. EcoCharge shows you the split for each charge, so the impact isn't just a claim — it's a number you can see for your own driving.
