Everything you need at the charger

One app to find a station, plug in, and see exactly what your charge cost — in money and in carbon.

Find a station

Search the map for solar-powered chargers near you, filtered by connector type and live availability.

Reserve, then scan

Hold your spot ahead of time, then scan the code on the charger to start your session in seconds.

Watch it charge

Follow your session live, from plug-in to full, and look back through your full charging history.

Pay from your wallet

Top up once and every session is paid automatically — no card to tap at the charger.

See your impact

Every session shows how much of your charge came from the sun, and what that saved.

Your profile, remembered

Save your vehicle and connector type once, and every search is filtered to fit.

Where the electrons actually come from

Each EcoCharge station generates its own power on-site: solar panels feed a battery bank through the day, and that stored charge is what fills your car — not just whatever's on the grid at that moment.

The app shows this split for every session, so you know how much of your charge was sun rather than substation.

Questions about installing

Why isn't EcoCharge on the Play Store?

We're sharing it directly for now. The app itself doesn't change — you're just installing it from a downloaded file instead of the Play Store app.

Is it safe to install an app this way?

Yes, as long as it comes from a source you trust. Android's "unknown sources" prompt exists so you consciously approve it — it's the same install step every app goes through, just without the store in between.

Will I get updates?

New versions will be posted on this same page. Since it's not installed through the Play Store, updates won't happen automatically — check back here for the latest APK.

What does the app need access to?

Just an internet connection, to talk to stations and your account in real time.
